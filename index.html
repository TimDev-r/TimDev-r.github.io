03 Try it: motion signatures

Two algorithms from the repositories above, running here in your browser — the same logic, ported to JavaScript.

Motion signatures

From motion-groundtruth-tools. It bins movement by distance from the camera into 128 characters, so a long recording becomes scannable text. Drag the subject, or change its distance.

3.0 m
0 m2 m4 m6 m8 m

  • - nothing moved
  • x micro-motion
  • + movement

Notice two bands light up. Depth is read from the current frame, so a moving subject marks its own distance and the wall it just uncovered — a disocclusion trail. It's inherent to differencing against a single depth map, and it's the first thing that confuses anyone reading a signature.

Cross-camera matching

From realsense-multicam-detection. One person, two cameras. Each deprojects its own bounding-box centre to 3D; if the two points land within 10 cm of each other, it's the same person. Drag the subject, and try widening the baseline.

With calibration off, the separation never drops below the baseline — because depth_matching.py compares each camera's own 3D points without transforming them into a shared frame. Push the baseline past 10 cm and matching fails for a person both cameras can see perfectly well. Tick Apply calibration and the stored R, T collapses the gap to sensor noise. That's the open issue named in the repo's README, made visible.
